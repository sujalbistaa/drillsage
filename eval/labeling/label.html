Question for every line: is this activity part of, or evidence of, a drilling problem that actually happened? Tick every hazard that applies. Lines that handle an ongoing problem (for example the trips of a fishing job) count. Drills, tests, precautions, plans and negated statements ("no losses", "well static") are No problem. If a depth for the problem is stated, enter it. Use Unsure rather than guessing: unsure items are left out of the metrics.
